Privacy Policy for GameMaster

Last Updated: August 22, 2026

At GameMaster, we attach great importance to the privacy of our users and the security of their data. This Privacy Policy explains what data our application collects, how it uses it, and your rights.

1. Data Collected

Our application may collect the following data groups to improve service quality:

  • Library Information: When you sync your game libraries (Steam, Epic Games, GOG, Amazon, Xbox, EA, Ubisoft), an authentication session token (cookie) issued by that platform is stored securely on your device to keep your library synced. Your passwords are entered directly on the respective platform's own login page and are never seen, transmitted, or stored by us.
  • Device Information: Device model, operating system version, crash and diagnostic logs, and Advertising ID.
  • Age Range Signals: To comply with applicable age verification regulations (such as the Texas SCOPE Act / SB 2420), our app queries Google Play's Age Signals API, which may return an age range associated with your Google Play account. This data is used only for regulatory compliance checks and is not stored or transmitted by us.

2. Purpose of Data Use

The collected data is used for the following purposes:

  • Keeping your connected game libraries synchronized and up to date.
  • Delivering game deals, free game listings, and search results.
  • Advertisements: Personalizing and optimizing in-app advertisements (via Google AdMob).
  • Verification of in-app purchases (e.g., removing ads).
  • Complying with applicable age verification laws.

3. Permissions

The following device permissions are requested and are each tied directly to a core feature of the app:

  • Internet (INTERNET) & Network State (ACCESS_NETWORK_STATE): required to sync your connected game libraries, fetch deals/free game listings, verify purchases, and serve advertisements.
  • Advertising ID (com.google.android.gms.permission.AD_ID): required so Google AdMob can serve and measure advertisements, including personalized ads where you have consented (see Section 3 below).

GameMaster does not request access to your microphone, camera, contacts, location, photos, or files.

4. Advertisements and Third-Party Services

Our app uses Google AdMob to display advertisements. This service provider may use your device's Advertising ID to show you advertisements tailored to your interests, where permitted by your consent choices.

Users can disable "Personalized Ads" at any time through their device settings, and EU/UK/Swiss users can manage their ad consent preferences from within the app (Profile → Privacy Settings).

For more information on how Google collects and uses data from apps that use its services, please visit: policies.google.com/technologies/partner-sites

For Google's general privacy policy, please visit: policies.google.com/privacy

5. Data Retention

Your data is retained only for as long as necessary to fulfill the purposes of this policy. Session tokens for connected libraries (Steam, Epic Games, GOG, Amazon, Xbox, EA, Ubisoft) remain stored on your device until you disconnect the account or uninstall the app. You can disconnect any connected account and remove its stored session data at any time from the app's Settings screen. Diagnostic and crash data is retained according to our service providers' standard retention periods.

6. Data Security

Third-party library synchronizations (Steam, Epic Games, GOG, Amazon, Xbox, EA, Ubisoft) are performed using the official and secure methods of the respective platforms. We take reasonable technical and organizational measures to protect your information from unauthorized access, alteration, disclosure, or destruction; however, no method of electronic transmission or storage is 100% secure.

7. In-App Purchases

Payments for removing ads or premium features are processed entirely through the Google Play Billing System. Your credit card or payment information is not processed or stored by us.

8. Your Privacy Rights

8.1 Ad Personalization (All Users)

You can review and change your advertising consent preferences at any time from Profile → Privacy Settings in the app. Regardless of your location, you can also control ad personalization at the device level via Android Settings → Privacy → Ads → "Delete advertising ID" or "Opt out of Ads Personalization."

8.2 GDPR (European Economic Area / UK)

If you are located in the EEA or UK, you have the right to: access, correct, or delete your personal data; restrict or object to processing; data portability; and to withdraw consent at any time (including for personalized advertising) without affecting the lawfulness of processing before withdrawal. You also have the right to lodge a complaint with your local data protection authority.

8.3 CCPA/CPRA (California, USA)

If you are a California resident, you have the right to: know what personal information is collected about you; request deletion of personal information; and opt out of the "sale" or "sharing" of personal information (as those terms are defined under CCPA/CPRA). We do not sell your personal information. Advertising identifiers may be considered "shared" for cross-context behavioral advertising purposes under CPRA; you can limit this using the ad personalization controls described in Section 8.1, or via Global Privacy Control (GPC) signals where supported.

8.4 Other Jurisdictions

If you reside in a jurisdiction with similar data protection laws (e.g., Brazil's LGPD, Canada's PIPEDA), you may have comparable rights. Please contact us using the details in Section 10 to exercise any applicable rights.

9. Children's Privacy

Our application targets users aged 18 and over. We do not knowingly collect personal data from children. If you believe a child has provided us with personal information, please contact us using the details below so we can address it.

These third parties maintain appropriate safeguards (such as Standard Contractual Clauses, where applicable) to protect your information in accordance with applicable data protection laws.
