Privacy Policy for Kelime Meydanı

Effective Date: August 22, 2026

Nyvrane ("we", "us", or "our") operates the Kelime Meydanı mobile application (the "Service").

This page informs you of our policies regarding the collection, use, and disclosure of personal data when you use our Service and the choices you have associated with that data.

We use your data to provide and improve the Service. By using the Service, you agree to the collection and use of information in accordance with this policy.

1. Information Collection and Use

We collect several different types of information for various purposes to provide and improve our Service to you.

Personal Data

While using our Service, we may ask you to provide us with certain personally identifiable information that can be used to contact or identify you ("Personal Data"). Personally identifiable information may include, but is not limited to:

  • Google Play Games Account Information: We use Google Play Game Services for authentication and leaderboard features. This may include your Gamer ID and profile information.
  • Game Data: To provide leaderboards and competitive features, we store your game scores, progress, and rankings on Firestore and Realtime Database.
  • Microphone and Audio Data: Microphone access may be required for users to utilize the voice response feature. The received audio data is only used for instantaneous speech-to-text conversion; audio recordings are not stored on your device or our servers, nor are they shared with third parties.

Usage Data

We may also collect information that your browser sends whenever you visit our Service or when you access the Service by or through a mobile device ("Usage Data"). This Usage Data may include information such as your device's Internet Protocol address (e.g., IP address), device type, unique device identifiers, and other diagnostic data.

2. Third-Party Service Providers

We may employ third-party companies and individuals to facilitate our Service ("Service Providers"), to provide the Service on our behalf, to perform Service-related services, or to assist us in analyzing how our Service is used.

These third parties have access to your Personal Data only to perform these tasks on our behalf and are obligated not to disclose or use it for any other purpose.

The Service uses the following third-party services:

  • Google Play Services: policies.google.com/privacy
  • AdMob (Google) — Used to display advertising: support.google.com/admob/answer/6128543
  • Google Analytics for Firebase — Used to understand app usage: firebase.google.com/policies/analytics
  • Firebase Crashlytics — Used for crash reporting and stability: firebase.google.com/terms/crashlytics

3. Advertising

We may use third-party Service Providers to show advertisements to you to help support and maintain our Service.

  • AdMob by Google: AdMob is an advertising service provided by Google Inc. You can opt out from the AdMob service by following the instructions described by Google: support.google.com/ads/answer/2662922

4. Permissions

The following device permissions are requested and are each tied directly to a core feature of the app:

  • Microphone (RECORD_AUDIO): required for the voice response feature described in Section 1 — spoken answers are converted to text instantaneously and are not retained.
  • Internet (INTERNET) & Network State (ACCESS_NETWORK_STATE): required to sync leaderboards and game data, serve advertisements, and connect to Google Play Game Services.
  • Vibration (VIBRATE): required for haptic feedback during gameplay.

Sharing a score image to apps like Instagram or TikTok uses Android's built-in file-sharing mechanism (FileProvider) and does not require any additional permission or send data to us.

5. Leaderboards and Public Profiles

If you choose to use the leaderboard features, your score and Google Play Games profile name will be visible to other players of Kelime Meydanı. By participating in these features, you consent to the public display of this information.

6. Account and Data Deletion

You may delete your account and all associated cloud data at any time.

  • In-App: Open the app, go to Settings ("Ayarlar"), and tap "Hesabımı ve Verilerimi Sil" ("Delete My Account and Data"). Confirming this action permanently deletes your Google Play Games-linked profile data, game scores, and leaderboard entries stored on our Firestore database. This action cannot be undone.
  • By Email: If you are unable to use the in-app option, you may request deletion of your account and associated data by contacting us at mind1060@gmail.com. We will process your request within a reasonable timeframe.

Please note that some information may be retained in backup or log form as required by law, or for legitimate business purposes such as fraud prevention, even after account deletion.

7. Data Retention

Your Google Play Games-linked profile data, game scores, and leaderboard entries are retained in Firestore/Realtime Database for as long as your account remains active, and are deleted upon request as described in Section 6. Diagnostic, crash, and analytics data collected by Firebase is retained according to Google/Firebase's standard retention periods. Microphone audio is never retained — it is converted to text instantaneously and discarded (see Section 1).

8. Data Security

The security of your data is important to us, but remember that no method of transmission over the Internet, or method of electronic storage is 100% secure. While we strive to use commercially acceptable means to protect your Personal Data, we cannot guarantee its absolute security.
