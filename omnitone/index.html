Privacy Policy for OmniTone

Effective Date: August 24, 2026  ·  Last Updated: August 24, 2026

This Privacy Policy describes how Nyvrane ("we," "us," or "our") collects, uses, and discloses information when you use the OmniTone mobile application (the "App"). By downloading, installing, or using OmniTone, you agree to the collection and use of information in accordance with this policy.

OmniTone is a music utility application that provides instrument tuning, a metronome, and audio analysis (tempo, time signature, chord, and scale detection) features.

1. Information We Collect

1.1 Audio Data (Processed On-Device)

OmniTone accesses your device's microphone to provide its core tuning, metronome-listening, and audio-analysis features. Audio captured for real-time tuning is processed entirely on your device and is never recorded, stored, or transmitted anywhere.

If you choose to record audio within the app (for the Master Analysis feature) or import an audio file from your device, that audio file is stored locally on your device only and analyzed entirely on-device using an on-device machine learning model. We do not upload, transmit, or have any server-side access to your audio recordings or imported audio files. You may delete these recordings/imports at any time from within the app.

1.2 Diagnostic and Crash Data

We use Firebase Crashlytics to automatically collect crash reports and diagnostic logs when the app crashes or encounters an error. This may include device model, operating system version, app version, and anonymized technical logs describing the state of the app at the time of the crash. This information is used solely to identify and fix bugs.

1.3 Usage and Analytics Data

We use Firebase Analytics to collect anonymized, aggregated usage data (such as which features are used and how often) to help us understand how the app is used and to improve it. This data is not used to personally identify you.

1.4 Advertising Identifiers

OmniTone displays advertisements through Google AdMob. AdMob and its partners may collect and use your Advertising ID (AAID) and other device/technical identifiers to serve and measure ads, including personalized ads where you have consented. See Section 4 ("Advertising and Your Choices") for details on how to control this.

1.5 Purchase Data

If you subscribe to OmniTone Pro, the subscription purchase is processed entirely by Google Play Billing. We do not collect, see, or store your payment card details, billing address, or other financial information — that data is handled directly by Google Play in accordance with Google's own privacy policy. We only receive a purchase/subscription status token used to unlock the ad-free experience on your device.

1.6 Age Signals

OmniTone uses Google Play's Age Signals API on supported devices to receive an approximate age-range signal, used solely to support compliance with regional age-verification and age-appropriate-experience requirements (such as jurisdiction-specific age-verification laws). We do not store this signal on any server we control; it is managed by Google Play and governed by Google's Privacy Policy.

1.7 Information We Do NOT Collect

OmniTone does not require you to create an account and does not collect your name, email address, phone number, or other directly identifying personal information. We do not access your contacts, location, photos, or files other than the specific audio file you explicitly choose to import for analysis.

2. Permissions

The following device permissions are requested and are each tied directly to a core feature of the app:

  • Microphone (RECORD_AUDIO): required for real-time instrument tuning, the metronome's listen/tap features, and in-app audio recording for analysis.
  • Internet (INTERNET): required to serve advertisements, verify your subscription status with Google Play, and send diagnostic/analytics data described in Sections 1.2–1.3.
  • Vibration (VIBRATE): required for the metronome's haptic beat feedback.

OmniTone does not request access to your contacts, location, camera, photos, files (other than an audio file you explicitly pick), call logs, or SMS.

3. How We Use Information

We use the information described above to:

  • Provide, operate, and maintain the App's core features
  • Diagnose and fix crashes and technical issues
  • Understand aggregate usage patterns to improve the App
  • Display and measure advertising (for users who have not subscribed to OmniTone Pro)
  • Process and validate subscription purchases
  • Comply with applicable laws and regulatory requirements

5. Advertising and Your Choices

OmniTone is monetized through advertising for users who have not purchased the OmniTone Pro subscription. Advertisements are served via Google AdMob.

5.1 Consent (EEA, UK, and Switzerland)

If you are located in the European Economic Area, the United Kingdom, or Switzerland, you will be presented with a consent form (via Google's User Messaging Platform) before any personalized advertising is shown. You can change your consent choice at any time from Profile → Privacy Options within the App.

5.2 Ad Personalization Controls

Regardless of your location, you can control ad personalization at the device level via Android Settings → Privacy → Ads → "Delete advertising ID" or "Opt out of Ads Personalization."

5.3 Age-Based Restriction (18+)

OmniTone's App Store listing designates a minimum audience age of 18 and over. This restriction relates exclusively to how advertising is served and personalized under applicable platform policies (e.g., restricting interest-based/personalized advertising to adult audiences) — it does not indicate that the App contains mature, violent, sexual, or otherwise age-restricted content. OmniTone's actual content (a music tuning, metronome, and audio-analysis tool) is suitable for a general audience; the 18+ designation is solely an advertising-eligibility classification. OmniTone is not directed at children, and we do not knowingly collect personal information from children. See Section 8 for more on children's privacy.

6. Data Storage and Security

  • Audio recordings, imported files, and analysis results are stored locally on your device (and, for subscription status, in a small local preference file). They are not transmitted to us or stored on any server we control.
  • Any network communication performed by third-party SDKs integrated into the App (advertising, analytics, crash reporting, billing) is encrypted in transit using industry-standard protocols (HTTPS/TLS).
  • We take reasonable technical and organizational measures to protect information from unauthorized access, alteration, disclosure, or destruction. However, no method of electronic transmission or storage is 100% secure, and we cannot guarantee absolute security.

7. Data Retention

  • Locally stored audio recordings, imports, and analysis results remain on your device until you delete them (via the App's "Delete" option) or uninstall the App.
  • Diagnostic, crash, and analytics data collected by Firebase is retained according to Google/Firebase's standard retention periods, as described in Firebase's own documentation and privacy policy.
  • If you subscribe and later cancel, your subscription status data is retained only as long as necessary to reflect your current entitlement, consistent with Google Play Billing's own data practices.

8. Children's Privacy

OmniTone is not directed at, marketed to, or intended for use by children under the age of 13 (or the applicable minimum age in your jurisdiction), and its Play Store listing designates a minimum audience of 18+ for advertising-eligibility purposes (see Section 5.3). We do not knowingly collect personal information from children. If you believe a child has provided us with personal information, please contact us using the information in Section 10 so we can take appropriate action, including deletion of such information.
